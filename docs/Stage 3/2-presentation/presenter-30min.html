03

One equation, three unknowns, eighteen years

Presenter 10:55The problem

What to say

Given only y, infinitely many splits into x + v are arithmetically valid. Every method in this talk supplies the missing information in a different way — that supplied information is called the prior, and the whole story is about where it lives. The equation on screen — y — what the sensor produced · x — the clean image we want · v — the noise. The classical answer writes the prior down as a formula: minimise ½‖y − x‖² + λ·Φ(x). You can read Φ, differentiate it, and argue about it. The deep answer replaces Φ with a tensor of learned weights. You cannot read it, but you can download it and it runs in 60 ms. The self-supervised answer takes the prior partly from the test image itself, so no clean data is needed at all. Where the prior lives, and who writes it, is the single axis this whole talk runs along.

Delivery

This is the only theory slide. Everything else is evolution. Do not derive MAP here — the master deck has it and the audience does not need it to follow the story.

What 'state of the art' means in this talk

Presenter 10:38Ground rules

What to say

The phrase is used loosely in most presentations. We use it with three qualifiers attached, because that is what makes the SOTA comparison later in the talk meaningful rather than rhetorical. A SOTA claim needs three things. A benchmark and a metric. 'Best denoiser' is meaningless. 'Highest PSNR on BSD68 at σ=25' is a claim you can check. A comparison protocol. Two papers reporting on the same dataset can still disagree, so wherever a row compares two methods we take both numbers from one table. Honesty about what is not reported. No paper here states how long it held a position, so this talk does not claim SOTA windows — only the publication date and which table each method leads. Which is why there is no single winner. Different benchmarks measure different capabilities: BSD68 measures average-case Gaussian removal, Urban100 measures long-range self-similarity, SIDD measures real sensor noise. A method can lead one and trail another. Restormer, NAFNet and MambaIR each lead a different table today. We return to this on slide 36 with the numbers, because 'why are there several SOTAs' is the question this talk is built to answer.

06

The innovation: filter patches together, not alone

Presenter 11:12BM3D · 2007 · the baseline

What to say

Dabov et al., IEEE TIP 2007. Previous transform-domain methods shrank each patch independently. BM3D's innovation is to group similar patches first, creating a third axis along which the signal is nearly constant and the noise is not. What changed. Block-match similar patches into a 3-D stack, transform the stack, hard-threshold, invert, aggregate — then repeat using the cleaner estimate as a Wiener pilot. Why it works. The paper's own ablation: even a transform that is random apart from the DC loses only 0.1–0.4 dB. The grouping does the work, not the transform. Impact. BSD68 σ=25: 28.57 dB at 2.85 s per 512×512. Became the reference line every paper for a decade had to beat. Still in use. Appears as a live baseline in Noise2Noise (2018), Noise2Void (2019), ZS-N2N (2023) and Restormer (2022) — a seventeen-year-old algorithm is still what new zero-shot methods must beat, because it needs no training data. Applications. The zero-shot comparison in those same papers, and the fallback where there is neither training data nor a GPU. On real sensor noise it still beats an AWGN-trained DnCNN by 1.99 dB.

Delivery

The random-transform ablation is the memorable fact — lead with it if pressed for time.

WNNM vs BM3D — the same grouping, a better estimator

What to say

Gu et al., CVPR 2014. WNNM keeps BM3D's non-local grouping and replaces what happens to the group. This is the first clean Before → After in the story, and it is entirely inside one step. The table compares Aspect, BM3D (2007), WNNM (2014), Why it matters. The row that matters is Performance: 28.57 dB · 2.85 s; 28.83 dB · 773.2 s; +0.26 dB for a 271× slowdown. This row is the whole argument for what comes next. Seven years of hand-designed priors bought 0.26 dB on BSD68 at σ=25, and the best method became 271× slower than the second best. The era had hit a wall in quality and cost simultaneously.

08

Why the era ended — stated by the paper that ended it

Presenter 10:38Bottleneck 1

What to say

DnCNN's introduction names the classical era's drawbacks in its own words. Quoting the paper that broke the bottleneck, rather than asserting it ourselves, is what makes this an evidenced timeline. The prior is paid for once per image. Prior-based methods involve a complex optimization problem in the testing stage. Nothing learned on image 1 helps image 2. WNNM: 773 s, every time. The prior is written by hand. The models are generally non-convex and involve several manually chosen parameters — block size, thresholds, λ, different values above and below σ=40. Its form limits the ceiling. Performance is inherently restricted to the specified forms of prior. You cannot exceed what your formula can express, however well you tune it. One model per noise level. Bridge methods train a specific model for a certain noise level and are limited in blind image denoising. In a real camera, σ is never known.

12

The innovation is eight characters

Presenter 11:32DnCNN · the implementation change

What to say

cszn/KAIR, models/network_dncnn.py, by DnCNN's own first author. The architecture is a list comprehension; the contribution is the return statement. On screen is the actual change, before and after. Why that one change matters. From ResNet: a mapping close to the identity is hard to optimise. And y is much more like x than it is like v — so learning the image forces the network toward an identity transform, which seventeen ReLU layers have no natural way to express. Learning the noise moves the trivial solution to zero, reachable by driving weights toward zero. Same function class, far easier optimisation landscape. The synergy, in one sentence. Residual learning strips the image out of the hidden layers, making activations Gaussian-like and content-independent — which is exactly the regime batch-norm's batch statistics assume. Without residual learning, BN has a certain adverse effect on convergence. Where the prior went. Nowhere in this file. It is in the.pth checkpoint this class will load. That is the moment the prior stopped being readable — and started being downloadable.

17

A theorem worth one conditional

Presenter 21:04Noise2Noise · 2018 · before → after

What to say

Lehtinen et al., ICML 2018. The most striking ratio in this story: a substantial statistical result whose implementation change is a single branch on which tensor becomes the target. On screen is the actual change, before and after. Why it works. L2 regression learns the conditional mean of the target. The paper states the consequence as a triviality: on expectation, the estimate remains unchanged if we replace the targets with random numbers whose expectations match the targets. So a second noisy photograph of the same scene is a valid target, because the noise is zero-mean. Given infinite data the solution is identical to clean-target training; for finite data the extra variance shrinks with dataset size. What it does NOT need: no likelihood model of the corruption, no density model of clean images. Measured cost: 0.02 dB. Kodak/BSD300/Set14 at σ=25 — clean targets 31.63, noisy targets 31.61. The clean-data requirement was never actually necessary.

One number proves the receptive field is the problem

Presenter 20:56Bottleneck 3 · the receipt

What to say

Identical models, identical table, identical noise level. The only thing that changes is the dataset — and the gain changes by a factor of five. +0.39 dB, BSD68, σ=50 · DnCNN → Restormer. Attention becomes affordable by changing one axis

Presenter 21:14Restormer · 2022 · before → after

What to say

Zamir et al., CVPR 2022. Standard self-attention costs O(W²H²) — a 256×256 image needs a 4.3-billion-entry attention matrix, against 196 tokens for a ViT. Restormer's innovation is to attend over channels instead of pixels. On screen is the actual change, before and after. One einops pattern is the entire complexity claim. Putting pixels on the last axis makes q @ k.transpose(-2,-1) a C×C matrix — 48×48 whether the image is 256² or 4000×3000. Cost becomes linear in pixels. Is it really global? Honestly: not pairwise-pixel. Every channel is computed from every pixel, so channel statistics carry image-wide information; the paper says global relationships are implicitly modelled and never claims otherwise. The depth-wise convolutions are load-bearing, not decoration. Removing qkv_dwconv costs 0.15 dB on Urban100 σ=50 — more than most architecture papers gain in total. Why SOTA: first method past 40 dB on both SIDD and DND; leads Urban100 at every noise level; 3.14× fewer FLOPs and 13× faster than SwinIR. Published at CVPR 2022; how long it holds those tables is not something any source states.

The paper that asked whether any of it was necessary

Presenter 21:14NAFNet · 2022 · before → after

What to say

Chen et al., ECCV 2022. While everyone added mechanisms, NAFNet removed them and scored higher. Its derivation is the most elegant reasoning in the modern half of this story. On screen is the actual change, before and after. The derivation. GELU is a special case of a gated linear unit with f, g = identity and σ = Φ. But a GLU itself contains nonlinearity and does not depend on σ — f(X)⊙g(X) is nonlinear because a product of two linear maps is not linear. So delete the activation; the gate already supplies it. Apply the same argument to channel attention and both its nonlinearities vanish too. Result: no Sigmoid, no ReLU, no GELU, no Softmax anywhere — the first work demonstrating that the nonlinear activation functions may not be necessary for SOTA computer vision methods. Both simplifications improved the score. SimpleGate beats GELU (+0.08), SCA beats CA (+0.03) on SIDD. The uncomfortable finding in their own ablation: the largest single gain is LayerNorm, +0.44 dB — because it stabilises training at a 10× larger learning rate. A large share of reported architectural progress in restoration is optimisation progress wearing a costume. Why SOTA: 40.30 dB on SIDD — the highest real-noise result in our comparison — at less than half the previous best's compute. Published at ECCV 2022. And by far the simplest code to maintain.

Four reasons the field has not converged on one method

Presenter 21:02Why several SOTAs

What to say

This is the question the professor is most likely to ask, so it gets its own slide rather than a sentence. The benchmarks measure different things. BSD68 measures average-case Gaussian removal and has saturated — 0.36 dB across five years. Urban100 measures long-range self-similarity. SIDD and DND measure real sensor noise. A method can lead one and trail another without any contradiction. The cost axes disagree with each other. Our own measurements give three incompatible rankings of the same models: by parameters DnCNN wins; by CPU wall-clock the P2N backbone wins; by real-noise quality NAFNet wins. Which one is 'best' depends entirely on what you are shipping onto what hardware. Supervision availability partitions the field. Branch A and branch B answer different questions. Where clean pairs exist, supervision wins by 1.35 dB. Where they do not, the self-supervised methods are not worse — they are the only option, and the supervised SOTA is simply unavailable.

What eighteen years did not solve

Presenter 20:44Open problems

What to say

Each of these is grounded in a documented limitation from the papers themselves or in our own measurements — not speculation. A blind denoiser for real cameras. SCUNet's abstract states it remains unsolved. Neither synthetic degradation nor SIDD-trained models generalise to an unseen camera with an unseen ISP, and no benchmark currently measures that. The evolution, in one paragraph

Presenter 20:40Conclusion

What to say

Denoising is the cleanest case study in how computer vision replaced hand-designed priors with learned ones, because here the prior is written down explicitly and you can watch it move. Four eras, three bottlenecks. Manual prior design ended when DnCNN made the prior a downloadable file. That created two new debts — it needed clean data, and it could only see 35 pixels — and in 2018 the field forked to pay them off separately. Neither branch has adopted the other's advance. Anticipated questions

Why are there several state-of-the-art methods rather than one best?
English

Because the benchmarks measure different capabilities and the cost axes disagree. BSD68 measures average-case Gaussian removal and has saturated — 0.36 dB across five years. Urban100 measures long-range self-similarity. SIDD and DND measure real sensor noise. NAFNet leads SIDD at 40.30 dB, Restormer leads Urban100 and is the only method past 40 dB on both real-noise sets, MambaIR leads DND by a hundredth of a decibel, and SCUNet competes on the training distribution instead of the architecture. A method can lead one table and trail another with no contradiction. On top of that our own measurements give three incompatible efficiency rankings of the same models.

پاسخ فارسی

چون محک‌ها توانایی‌های متفاوتی را می‌سنجند و محورهای هزینه با هم نمی‌خوانند. BSD68 حذف نویز گاوسی در حالت میانگین را می‌سنجد و اشباع شده — ۰٫۳۶ دسی‌بل در پنج سال. Urban100 خودتشابهی دوربرد را می‌سنجد. SIDD و DND نویز واقعی حسگر را. NAFNet در SIDD با ۴۰٫۳۰ پیشتاز است، Restormer در Urban100 و تنها روشی است که در هر دو محک نویز واقعی از ۴۰ دسی‌بل گذشته، MambaIR در DND با اختلاف یک‌صدم دسی‌بل جلوست، و SCUNet به‌جای معماری روی توزیع آموزشی رقابت می‌کند. یک روش می‌تواند در یک جدول اول باشد و در جدول دیگر عقب، بدون هیچ تناقضی. افزون بر این، اندازه‌گیری‌های خودمان سه رتبه‌بندی کارایی ناسازگار از همان مدل‌ها می‌دهد.

What exactly changed between DnCNN and the previous approach?
English

Three things, and the paper is explicit about each. First, the architecture stops resembling an optimiser — TNRD unrolled a PDE into stages; DnCNN is a plain Conv-BN-ReLU stack with no pooling. Second, the network outputs the noise rather than the image, so the clean result is y minus the prediction. Third, residual learning and batch normalisation are used together, and the paper shows neither works nearly as well alone. In code the contribution is the return statement: return x minus n instead of return the model output.

پاسخ فارسی

سه چیز، و مقاله دربارهٔ هر سه صریح است. اول، معماری دیگر شبیه یک بهینه‌ساز نیست — TNRD یک معادلهٔ دیفرانسیل را به مراحل باز کرده بود؛ DnCNN یک پشتهٔ سادهٔ Conv-BN-ReLU بدون ادغام است. دوم، شبکه به‌جای تصویر، نویز را خروجی می‌دهد، پس نتیجهٔ تمیز برابر است با y منهای پیش‌بینی. سوم، یادگیری پسماند و نرمال‌سازی دسته‌ای با هم به کار می‌روند و مقاله نشان می‌دهد هیچ‌کدام به‌تنهایی نزدیک به این نتیجه نمی‌رسند. در کد، مشارکت اصلی همان دستور return است: به‌جای بازگرداندن خروجی مدل، x منهای n برگردانده می‌شود.

Why was predicting the noise important rather than just a stylistic choice?
English

Because it changes the optimisation landscape without changing the function class. From ResNet, a mapping close to the identity is hard to optimise. The noisy image is much more like the clean image than it is like the noise, so learning the image forces the network toward an identity transform, which a stack of seventeen ReLU convolutions has no natural way to express. Learning the noise instead moves the do-nothing solution to zero, which is trivially reachable by driving weights toward zero.

پاسخ فارسی

چون چشم‌انداز بهینه‌سازی را عوض می‌کند بدون آنکه کلاس تابعی عوض شود. از ResNet می‌دانیم نگاشتی که به همانی نزدیک است سخت بهینه می‌شود. تصویر نویزی بسیار بیشتر به تصویر تمیز شبیه است تا به نویز، پس یادگیری تصویر شبکه را به‌سمت یک نگاشت همانی می‌راند که پشته‌ای از هفده کانولوشن با ReLU راه طبیعی برای بیانش ندارد. در مقابل، یادگیری نویز راه‌حل «کاری نکن» را روی صفر می‌گذارد که با میل‌دادن وزن‌ها به صفر به‌سادگی قابل دستیابی است.

What makes Restormer state of the art, and since when?
English

Concretely: it is the first method past 40 dB on both SIDD and DND, it leads Urban100 at every noise level, and it reports 3.14 times fewer FLOPs and 13 times faster runtime than SwinIR. It was published at CVPR 2022. I would not claim a period over which it held that position — no paper here reports one, and I am not going to estimate it. What distinguishes it from NAFNet is where the advantage sits — Restormer's gain is concentrated on long-range repetitive structure, which is exactly what its channel attention was designed to recover, while NAFNet leads on real sensor noise with far simpler code.

پاسخ فارسی

مشخصاً: نخستین روشی است که در هر دو محک SIDD و DND از ۴۰ دسی‌بل عبور کرده، در Urban100 در همهٔ سطوح نویز پیشتاز است، و ۳٫۱۴ برابر FLOP کمتر و ۱۳ برابر سریع‌تر از SwinIR گزارش می‌دهد. در CVPR سال ۲۰۲۲ منتشر شده است. دربارهٔ اینکه چه مدت این جایگاه را نگه داشته ادعایی نمی‌کنم — هیچ‌کدام از مقالات چنین چیزی گزارش نکرده‌اند و ما هم تخمین نمی‌زنیم. آنچه آن را از NAFNet جدا می‌کند جای مزیت است — دستاورد Restormer روی ساختار تکرارشوندهٔ دوربرد متمرکز است که دقیقاً همان چیزی است که توجه کانالی‌اش برای بازیابی‌اش طراحی شده، در حالی که NAFNet روی نویز واقعی حسگر با کدی به‌مراتب ساده‌تر پیشتاز است.

Can you propose a concrete improvement based on these limitations?
English

Yes, and it falls straight out of the structural finding of this project: the two branches have never merged. P2N still ships the 2018 Noise2Noise U-Net, while Restormer, NAFNet and MambaIR all assume paired supervised data. As far as we found, nobody has trained a Transformer or state-space backbone under P2N's renoised-data supervision. It is cheap to test because both halves already exist as working code, and it is measurable: run Noise2Void's own protocol on BSD68 at sigma 25 and on the cryo-TEM sets, and see whether the 1.35 dB self-supervision gap shrinks.

پاسخ فارسی

بله، و مستقیماً از یافتهٔ ساختاری این پروژه می‌آید: دو شاخه هرگز به هم نپیوسته‌اند. P2N هنوز همان U-Net سال ۲۰۱۸ را عرضه می‌کند، در حالی که Restormer و NAFNet و MambaIR همگی دادهٔ زوجِ بانظارت را فرض می‌گیرند. تا جایی که ما یافتیم، کسی ستون فقرات ترنسفورمری یا فضای‌حالت را تحت نظارت دادهٔ دوباره‌نویزی‌شدهٔ P2N آموزش نداده است. آزمودنش کم‌هزینه است چون هر دو نیمه از قبل به‌صورت کد کارا وجود دارند، و قابل اندازه‌گیری است: پروتکل خودِ Noise2Void را روی BSD68 در سیگما ۲۵ و روی مجموعه‌های cryo-TEM اجرا کنید و ببینید آیا شکاف ۱٫۳۵ دسی‌بلی خودنظارتی کم می‌شود.

Why does the field still use BM3D as a baseline in 2023?
English

Because it needs no training data and no GPU, which makes it the only fair comparison for zero-shot methods. Noise2Noise, Noise2Void and ZS-N2N all benchmark against it for exactly that reason. And on real sensor noise it still beats an AWGN-trained DnCNN by 1.99 dB, because it never committed to a learned noise distribution in the first place — it only assumes local self-similarity, which stays true.

پاسخ فارسی

چون نه دادهٔ آموزشی می‌خواهد نه GPU، و همین آن را تنها مقایسهٔ منصفانه برای روش‌های zero-shot می‌کند. Noise2Noise و Noise2Void و ZS-N2N دقیقاً به همین دلیل با آن مقایسه می‌شوند. ضمناً روی نویز واقعی حسگر هنوز ۱٫۹۹ دسی‌بل از DnCNNِ آموزش‌دیده با AWGN بهتر است، چون از ابتدا به هیچ توزیع نویزِ آموخته‌شده‌ای متعهد نشده — فقط خودتشابهی محلی را فرض می‌گیرد که همچنان درست است.

What limitations remain in the current SOTA?
English

Four. A general blind denoiser for real cameras remains unsolved — SCUNet's abstract says so, and nothing generalises to an unseen camera with an unseen ISP. Fidelity metrics reward blur, because L1, L2 and PSNR all recover a conditional mean and Noise2Noise itself names the resulting spatial blurriness. Nobody agrees what efficient means. And the benchmarks have saturated: BSD68 moved 0.36 dB in five years while real noise moved sixteen.

پاسخ فارسی

چهار مورد. نویززدای کورِ همه‌منظوره برای دوربین‌های واقعی هنوز حل نشده — چکیدهٔ SCUNet همین را می‌گوید و هیچ روشی به دوربین دیده‌نشده با ISP دیده‌نشده تعمیم نمی‌یابد. معیارهای وفاداری به تاری پاداش می‌دهند، چون L1 و L2 و PSNR همگی میانگین شرطی را بازیابی می‌کنند و خودِ Noise2Noise تاری مکانی حاصل را نام می‌برد. کسی بر سر معنای «کارآمد» توافق ندارد. و محک‌ها اشباع شده‌اند: BSD68 در پنج سال ۰٫۳۶ دسی‌بل جابه‌جا شد در حالی که نویز واقعی شانزده دسی‌بل.
